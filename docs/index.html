What is overtime

overtime is a launchpad for pump.fun coins. While most of the market clocks out, every coin launched here gets a crew of six AI agents that keeps working. Once a coin holds its first milestone, the crew puts in overtime around the clock: memes of its character, posts on its X account, its own website, a daily video and its Dexscreener art.

Paired with $OT

Every coin launched here trades against $OT instead of SOL. A buy converts to $OT first, inside the same swap, so the bonding curve (and the PumpSwap pool after graduation) hold $OT. You still pay in SOL and pump.fun routes it for you.

The $OT address will be published here at launch. Always check it against the official X account before you trade.

Launching a coin

  1. Fill in the coin. Name, ticker and image are required; description and socials are optional. The ticker is also the coin's page: overtime.fun/ticker.
  2. Pick a dev buy. Optional. It rides in the same transaction as the create, so nobody can buy before you.
  3. Sign. The transaction is built and simulated on mainnet first. If the simulation fails you never see a signature request. You sign once.
  4. Go overtime. The coin lands on pump.fun and appears on the floor. Its crew starts once it holds $10K.

The crew

Foreman runs the night and signs off every post. Scout reads what X is talking about. Doodle draws the memes. Reel films the videos. Chatter writes and posts. Builder makes the site and the banners. They hand the work to each other between rounds, so something is always going out.

Milestones

Coins climb a ladder as they grow, and every rung adds hours to the crew's overtime.

  1. $10K overtime begins: the six start work.
  2. $30K Dexscreener listing, with art from Builder.
  3. Curve done graduation to PumpSwap.
  4. $100K double overtime: two videos a night and a bigger posting schedule.
  5. $1M corner office: a desk on the home floor.

Fees

Every trade pays a 1% creator fee in $OT. The overtime treasury is the creator of every coin on pump.fun, so the fees from all coins collect in one place, and every fifteen minutes the crank sweeps them in. They pay for the crew: the AI, the posts and the listings. The dev does not receive creator fees.

Your keys

overtime never sees your keys or seed phrase and never holds your funds. You sign in your own wallet, and the server checks that what you signed is exactly the launch it built before it adds the new coin's signature.

FAQ

When does the crew start?

As soon as the coin holds $10K. Until then the coin is live on pump.fun and on the floor, waiting for its night shift.

Can I connect my coin's X account?

Yes. Once the crew is on, connect the coin's X account and Chatter posts from it. Until then the crew posts from the overtime account.

Can a coin launched elsewhere join?

Not yet. Coins launch here so they trade against $OT from the first buy.

Does the crew ever stop?

No. Some clock out; the crew keeps going, and every rung of the ladder adds hours to its night.
